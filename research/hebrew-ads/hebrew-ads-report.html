The one-line answer

The open lane

אף כלי לא מייצר היום פרסומת של 30 שניות בעברית עם קריינות טבעית, כתוביות RTL נכונות, והקשר ישראלי — במחיר של עסק קטן. No tool today natively produces a polished 30-second Hebrew ad with a natural voice, correct RTL burned-in captions, and Israeli cultural context — at SMB prices. Global AI video tools treat Hebrew as a checkbox; Israeli production is expensive. That gap is the product.

And the repo is ~80% ready

A working Hebrew RTL commercial already exists — shorts/short-16-formy — proving the whole chain (edge-tts Hebrew voice → word-exact .words.json → RTL captions with vendored Hebrew fonts). The missing 20% is a /make-ad skill, three ad components, a bidi-safe number fix, and a louder ad style. Jump to the plan.

Where Israelis actually watch

A video-first, WhatsApp-centric market

Israel is young (median age 29.2), near-universally online (91.3% internet, 109% mobile connections), and 93.8% of adults use social media. Short vertical video is the dominant ad surface.

Design for sound-on

Hebrew audio carries tone, humor, and colloquialism that captions alone can't — so sound-on is the norm in Israel, the opposite of US silent-first design. The voiceover is the primary meaning layer; captions reinforce it.

The posting rhythm is shaped by the Jewish week

Weekday evenings (18:00–23:00) are the core window. Friday afternoon drops steeply into Shabbat. Saturday night after Shabbat (~20:00+) is the single peak scrolling window of the week. Never post on Shabbat, Yom Kippur, or Memorial Day.

What an Israeli SMB can and will pay

Budgets & price psychology

~700–730K active businesses, 68% one-person self-employed. SMBs produce ~55% of national output but access only ~24% of business credit — price-sensitive, ROI-focused, churn-prone. Their #1 and #2 pain points are time and money; 48% feel enslaved to their business.

SaaS price anchor: ₪50–60/month

  • ActiveTrail (10,000+ Israeli businesses) anchors the market at ₪50 Basic / ₪60 "popular".
  • Under ~₪100/mo = impulse-buy, card-on-file, treated like Netflix. Above ~₪150 it becomes a "business expense."
  • Norm: 14-day free trial, no card required, 15% off annual prepay.

The churn trap

  • The $25–50/mo band has the highest churn in B2B SaaS — 8.6% monthly.
  • AI-native tools under $50 retain only 23% of revenue; 60–70% of churn lands in the first 90 days.
  • Survival = habit loops: weekly WhatsApp video prompts, saved brand assets, visible view/lead counters.

The AI-trust caveat

Israelis adopt AI fast (28% of businesses, 2× the EU average) — but consumers are wary: fully-AI ads get +19% clicks, yet a "Created with AI" label cuts clicks ~31.5%, and the Coca-Cola AI ad drew real backlash in Israel. → Market "your products, AI-assisted" / authentic UGC-style, not obvious synthetic avatars.

The Hebrew of Israeli ads

Write like 9 million people talk

The rule: "ISRAELI > ACADEMY." Semiformal spoken Hebrew (תקנית-לייט) — never formal (אנו, הינך, לפיכך read as stiff and alienating), rarely pure street. Hebrish is expected (סייל, דיל, ברנד, אונליין).

The CTA & urgency toolkit

הזמינו עכשיוorder now — plural imperative, the default CTA חסכו 30% עכשיוsave 30% now משלוח חינם היוםfree shipping today עד גמר המלאיuntil stock runs out נגמר היום. 50% הנחהends today, 50% off — FOMO

The two magic phrases

בלי מנוי (no subscription) and חד פעמי (one-time payment) — Israelis hate recurring payments. And prices are always in ₪, never $.

The slogan formula

3–6 words, rhyming or punning, built on a Hebrew root verb or a naturalized English noun:

זה טוב, זה אסםOsem — puns on "strength" מה הקשר? בירה נשר!Nesher — entered slang ככה זה כשאוהביםCellcom — colloquial warmth

Gender is vertical-dependent, not universal

Masculine-plural (אתם) is the broad default — but it is actively wrong for women's beauty (use feminine-singular את). Barbershops & trades take masculine; restaurants & real estate take neutral/mixed. Bake a per-vertical register into the generator.

And never use ניקוד (vowel marks) in digital Hebrew — write ktiv maleh, short unambiguous words, Hebrew quotes (״ ״), gershayim abbreviations (סה"כ).

Hooks & spoken slang

The 3-second hook

Land the hook before 2 seconds or they scroll. Israeli openings are conversational imperatives and exclamations:

רגע, אתם חייבים לראות את זהwait, you have to see this סיפור מטורףan insane story אף אחד לא מדבר על זהno one's talking about this רגע, תקשיבו ליwait, listen to me

Energy markers (Arabic-derived, instantly native)

יאללהlet's go וואלהwow / really סבבהcool אחלהawesome תכלסbottom line חבל"זironic = amazing סחתןprops

Directness is a feature, not a bug: dugri (דוגרי) bluntness and tachles are trust signals. Fast, high-energy, first-person to-camera — "סבבה, בואו נדבר על…" Match the register to the audience: secular Tel Aviv is maximally slangy; national-religious adds blessings (ב"ה); Haredi avoids internet slang; Arab-Israeli leans on Arabic interjections.

The cultural code

Consumer psychology

Israeli consumer behavior is driven by a few deep codes. Get these right and the ad feels native; get them wrong and the freier-alarm goes off.

Never make the viewer a פרייר (freier/sucker). The "11th commandment." Frame every deal as proof the buyer is smart — "you beat the system" — not merely cheap. Show the math of the deal.

שווה ("worth it") beats "cheap." Israelis are value-maximizers. The 2011 cottage-cheese boycott (100K FB members, −30% demand, price reversed in days) made consumers durably price-militant. Justify the price.

Speak דוגרי from a named, visible owner. Polish reads as "someone's trying to fool me." The family shop (חנות של משפחה) and word-of-mouth (מפה לאוזן) out-trust any faceless brand.

Warmth + משפחתיות + nostalgia is the payload. The Bamba playbook — 25% of the snack market via a baby mascot and intergenerational memory.

Make the brand the customer's ally against profiteering (the Shufersal-vs-Tnuva playbook). Side with the customer in the price fight.

חוצפה + דווקא humor punches UP. Self-deprecate first, mock the situation (bureaucracy, traffic), never punch down at the customer.

Hard taboos — non-negotiable

No Holocaust, no terror victims, no fallen soldiers, no army-as-sales-device (the Cellcom 2009 lesson), no casual politics/religion. In wartime: sober, service-oriented, or "strategic silence."

Per-sector lexicons

Each vertical speaks its own Hebrew

The #1 product rule: each vertical loads its own vocabulary, offer structure, register, CTA, and proof type — generic ad-Hebrew reads as "not written for my business."

Restaurants / food

Register: neutral/mixed

Fixed-price packages with price-anchoring (ארוחה לזוג ב-165 ש"ח במקום 336), course vocabulary, transliterated dishes (אנטריקוט, צ'יפס), kosher/premium signals.

CTA: הזמנת שולחן / הזמנת מקום — not "תפוס מקום" (not idiomatic).

Beauty / salon (women)

Register: feminine singular

All persuasive copy feminine (אני מזמינה אותך, עצרי, בחרי); clinical vocabulary (אבחון, פרוטוקול, רטינול); concern→empathy→solution→invitation.

Proof: before/after as a narrative transformation line.

Salon / barbershop

Register: masculine

The appointment economy (קביעת תור, תזכרות אוטומטיות); first-visit perk, word-of-mouth discount (הנחה למעבירי מילת הפה), off-peak special.

Offers: first-visit perk, referral discount, off-peak deal.

Real estate

Register: neutral

Discount typology (הנחה אמיתית, הנחה פיננסית), payment splits (10%-90%), room-count sizing, the ממ"ד (safe room) selling point, anchor against מחיר מחירון.

CTA: buyer-empowerment — בדיקת עסקאות דומות.

Fitness / gyms

Register: energetic neutral

The offer is a trial (שיעור ניסיון חינם / שבוע התנסות) with real fine print as trust (מוגבל לשימוש 1 בחצי שנה).

Proof: לפני ואחרי (before/after) transformation.

Trades (plumber/electrician/AC)

Register: masculine

Emergency/speed (צנרת שהתפוצצה לא מחכה, מי שעונה ראשון לוקח את העבודה) + trust credentials (מאומתים, אחריות שנה-שנתיים) + tiered pricing.

CTA: השוו הצעות via one free WhatsApp inquiry.

Hebrew on screen

RTL, fonts & captions

  • ✓

    Burn captions in. Subtitles were always "part of the image" in Israel; dubbing never won. Max 2 lines per card, ~60 chars full-width, ktiv maleh, no nikud.

  • ✓

    Fonts designers use: Assistant, Heebo, Rubik, Alef, Varela Round (sans); Frank Ruhl (serif, premium); Secular One (display). All free, full Hebrew coverage.

  • !

    The #1 technical risk is bidi, not fonts. Trailing punctuation/percent/phone can jump to the wrong side. Fix = RTL base direction + isolate numbers/Latin. Test 50% הנחה, phone numbers, line-end !.

  • !

    Avoid per-character typewriter on Hebrew — it scrambles word order. Reveal whole line blocks, wipe right-to-left, slide in from the right.

  • ✓

    Caption style: bold weight, white/bright on a dark strip/heavy outline, bottom-third, emoji only at line start/end (1–3 max, e.g. 🔥 מבצע חם).

Stay legal

Israeli ad regulation

Bake these into the generator so an ad can't be published non-compliant:

  • §2

    Consumer Protection Law, Sec. 2 — no misleading the consumer on any material matter.

  • §15

    Sec. 15 — any special sale must disclose scope, terms, and the discount rate; show the total price online.

  • §7a

    Sec. 7a + 1991 regs — special protection against deceptive marketing to minors.

  • שת"פ

    Influencer/native ads must be visibly labeled פרסום בשיתוף פעולה / שת״פ ממומן up front, not buried in hashtags.

  • +

    Also: Privacy Protection Law Amendment 13 (in force Aug 2025) for customer data, and ACUM music licensing for commercial video.

Exact fine amounts weren't verifiable from gov.il in this environment (it blocks automated fetch) — confirm current figures before relying on them in an ad.

The harness

The plan, in build order

A /make-ad skill
Brief → interrogation for business{} → hook per vertical → Hebrew script.md + beats.json (register per line) → edge-tts voice → RTL captions → CTA end card → QA → SFX/music → cost report.
Three ad components
remotion/src/lib/ads.tsx: AdEndCard (phone + wa.me, safe-area-correct), PriceBadge (bidi-safe ₪, struck oldPrice, discount stamp), Logo/Watermark.
Bidi-safe numbers
formatILPrice() + isolated-digit phone formatter so 50%, 12,345 ₪, 050-123-4567 never reorder inside a Hebrew caption token.
Per-vertical lexicon templates
Each vertical loads its own vocabulary, register, offer structure, CTA, and proof type (section 7).
Holiday-aware template engine
Pick the ad angle from today's date on the Israeli calendar; pre-load campaigns 1–2 weeks ahead.
Ad style variant
An explicit mode:"ad" override: louder energy, higher-contrast stroke, bolder scale-pop, an urgency accent, a defined CTA-card ending. Keep calm premium for non-ad shorts.
Contract validation
validate_ad_beats() — required ad.business/offer/cta, numeric price, currency default ₪, a phoneDisplay or wa.me link, cta.type ∈ {phone, whatsapp, both, visit}.

Two deliberate rule overrides (ads ≠ shorts)

Drop the seamless loop

Ads are one-shot linear persuasion; the end card must hold so the phone/WhatsApp is tappable. An ad-mode exception, not style drift.

Override the no-CTA rule

The conversion CTA is the payoff for an ad — the end card replaces the "end on the payoff line" rule for mode:"ad" only.

Voice decision

Default to edge-tts (he-IL-AvriNeural / he-IL-HilaNeural) — free, unlimited, native word boundaries, already proven. ElevenLabs is an optional upgrade but only with --model eleven_multilingual_v3 --lang he (~$0.03–0.10/ad). Exclude kokoro for Hebrew entirely.

WhatsApp-native (onboarding, drafts, revision, view-notifications over WhatsApp; one-tap "forward to a fellow business owner" referral). Price ₪49 starter / ₪99 popular / ₪399 agency, 14-day free trial no card.
